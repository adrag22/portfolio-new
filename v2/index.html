HelloFresh 2023–Present

CDP → ML Scoring → AI Agent Orchestration for Global Marketing

Product Lead · Enterprise AI · Global
The Problem

Marketing teams across HelloFresh Group were making channel-mix and creative decisions on lagging indicators — long campaign lead times, siloed audience data, and no way to systematically match creative to intent signals across paid & organic channels.

The Bet

Build a customer data enrichment layer on the CDP feeding ML models that scored prospects and existing customers, orchestrated by an AI Agent layer that segmented audiences by channel and surfaced high-relevance campaign recommendations — with humans reviewing before launch.

How I Drove It

Aligned Data, ML, and Marketing leadership on the full pipeline. Translated technical architecture into commercial narratives for GTM adoption. Partnered with UX Design to prototype the decisioning + creative selection interface. Delivered strong positive Marketing team feedback.

GoTo Technologies 2021–2022

Unified Commerce & Pricing Restructure across Remote Support + Collaboration

Senior Product Manager · Platform · Berlin
The Problem

GoTo's Remote Support and Collaboration products lived in separate commerce silos — different pricing pages, checkout flows, and packaging. 7M+ enterprise users couldn't easily cross-buy, and the sales team had no unified motion.

The Bet

Design and roll out a unified commerce system that restructured pricing tiers across the entire product portfolio — enabling cross-sell, tier-migration, and a single enterprise contract surface. Simultaneously, build marketplace integrations to lock in stickiness.

How I Drove It

Coordinated Platform, Billing, and GTM to migrate legacy SKUs into the new taxonomy. Prioritized ISV integrations that made switching costly. Enabled multi-feed video + interactive whiteboarding for large meetings under weak network conditions — reducing customer-facing churn triggers.

Rakuten 2019–2021

Cost Center → $17M PaaS → $2M SaaS Extension in APAC

Product Manager II · Cloud Platform · Bangalore
The Problem

Rakuten's cloud platform served 1.9B members and 130+ developer teams, but was seen internally as a cost center. There was no external revenue narrative, and 70+ business units were locked into vendor-specific integrations.

The Bet

Architect API-first cloud services (Compute, Storage, Observability) with standardized schemas that decoupled teams from vendor lock-in — then commercialize the platform first as internal PaaS, then extend to APAC as external SaaS via SI/ISV Partner Program.

How I Drove It

Designed extensible data models and API contracts. Led GTM strategy and SI/ISV Partner Program to drive enterprise adoption. Enabled 130+ dev teams to self-serve instrumentation without custom integrations — dramatically improving deployment velocity across BUs.

Clear 2018–2019

Plug-n-Play ERP Connectors for 1,500+ SMBs

Product Manager · B2B Financial Accounting · Bangalore
The Problem

Indian SMBs struggled with GST compliance and working capital because they used fragmented ERP systems — Tally, SAP, Zoho, Oracle. Manual reconciliation, invoice corrections, and vendor follow-ups added weeks to every filing cycle.

The Bet

Build plug-n-play API connectors that integrate with every major ERP with >99% data ingestion accuracy — then layer automated invoice corrections and one-click vendor communication on top to slash friction.

How I Drove It

Prioritized ERPs by SMB market share. Standardized the integration contract to make onboarding a self-serve motion. Built automated invoice correction and vendor communication flows — reducing back-and-forth by an order of magnitude for finance teams.
