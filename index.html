Four deep dives into how I think about high-assurance AI — the problem, the bet, how I drove it, and what I'd do differently. The through-line: making model behavior safe, auditable, and commercially valuable inside regulated workflows.

HelloFresh · 2023–Present

Making GenAI Auditable: AI Governance & MCP Agent Orchestration

Product Lead — ML & GenAI · Global
The Problem

HelloFresh wanted GenAI in customer-facing and compliance-sensitive workflows, but had no way to deploy it safely. Hallucinations, opaque reasoning, and unbounded tool use are unacceptable under GDPR/PSD2-adjacent scrutiny — and there was no framework for evaluating, monitoring, or auditing model behavior.

The Bet

Build the company's first AI Governance framework and production guardrails from scratch — evaluation, drift detection, action-layer policy enforcement, audit-trace requirements — and an MCP-based orchestration platform where prompts, tools, model configs and policies are jointly versioned so every agent action is reconstructible.

How I Drove It

Led engineering and data science to stand up guardrails and standardized MCP integration patterns. Validated the ecosystem against synthetic-data environments before any live rollout, and made reproducible traces a hard requirement for root-cause analysis and audit.

First AI Governance framework + GenAI guardrails shipped
MCP orchestration: every agent action reconstructible for audit
Hallucination-resistant deployment in compliance workflows
Synthetic-data validation before live rollout

🔁 What I'd do differently: Version policies alongside prompts and models from day one. We retrofitted joint versioning after the fact — treating governance as an afterthought cost us reproducibility on the earliest agents.

HelloFresh · 2023–Present

Stopping €100M in Compliance Risk with ML Fraud Controls

Product Lead — ML & GenAI · Global
The Problem

A rising Account Takeover pattern was driving fraudulent orders and serious regulatory exposure. In parallel, fraudulent compensation claims and a novel payment-fraud pattern were draining millions — existing controls were blocking just 0.5% of malicious payments.

The Bet

Deploy ML-driven verification and detection models that separate bad actors from legitimate users at scale — across ATO, compensation fraud and payment fraud simultaneously — while keeping friction for genuine customers under 1%.

How I Drove It

Led engineering and data science across three parallel workstreams with model governance and monitoring built in from day one, so detection gains never came at the cost of auditability or customer experience.

€100M compliance penalty exposure avoided globally
>€9M churn losses reduced via ATO verification controls
>€4M annual compensation-fraud losses eliminated
Payment-fraud blocking: 0.5% → 56%
<1% friction for legitimate users maintained

🔁 What I'd do differently: Invest in a shared feature store earlier — we rebuilt similar features across models independently, costing weeks of iteration on each subsequent model.

Rakuten · 2019–2021

0→1 Observability Platform that became a $17M/yr PaaS

Product Manager II — Cloud Platform · Bangalore
The Problem

A global private cloud serving 70+ business units, 130+ developer teams and 1B+ members was seen as a cost center. Teams were locked into vendor-specific formats, and there was no self-serve path to instrumentation or a clear value proposition.

The Bet

Build an API-first, full-stack observability suite — Compute, Storage, Observability — with standardized configuration schemas that decouple teams from vendor formats, compelling enough to shift business units from a cost-center mindset to paying Platform-as-a-Service customers.

How I Drove It

Owned product strategy and delivery across engineering, PM and UX. Designed extensible data models and API contracts, led the GTM and an SI/ISV partner program, and extended the platform as a SaaS offering into the APAC market.

$17M/year saved via cost-center → PaaS shift
$2M ARR from SaaS extension into APAC
130+ developer teams self-serving instrumentation
Vendor lock-in eliminated across 70+ business units

🔁 What I'd do differently: Run a formal developer-experience survey at the 3-month mark. We learned too late that onboarding documentation — not feature gaps — was the #1 adoption blocker.

How I Drove It
