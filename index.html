HelloFresh · 2023–Present

Stopping €100M in Compliance Risk with ML Fraud Controls

Product Lead — ML & GenAI · Global
The Problem

Account Takeover attacks were costing €3M/year and creating serious regulatory exposure. Simultaneously, compensation fraud and a novel payment fraud pattern were draining millions—yet existing controls flagged <1% of malicious payments.

The Bet

Build a suite of ML-driven verification and detection models distinguishing bad actors from legitimate users at sub-1% false positive rates—at global scale, across multiple fraud vectors simultaneously.

How I Drove It

Led cross-functional squads across three parallel workstreams: ATO detection, compensation fraud automation, and payment fraud blocking. Established model governance and monitoring from day one.

€100M compliance penalty exposure avoided
€4M+ annual fraud losses eliminated
Payment fraud blocking: 0.5% → 56%
<1% friction for legitimate users

🔁 What I'd do differently: Invest in a shared feature store earlier — we rebuilt similar features across models independently, costing weeks of iteration time on each model.

Product Manager II — Cloud Platform · Bangalore
The Problem

130+ internal developer teams at Rakuten used fragmented monitoring tools. The cloud platform was a cost center—with 1.9B members depending on Rakuten's ecosystem, reliability was non-negotiable.

The Bet

Build a full-stack, API-first observability platform from scratch compelling enough to shift 70+ business units to a Platform-as-a-Service model.

How I Drove It

Led 3 cross-functional teams (engineers, PM, UX) through product-driven discovery and delivery. Architected API-first cloud services. Executed comprehensive GTM across on-prem and cloud.
